      Teacher guide

      One routine for every student.

      Keep the four exam parts and the original score weights. Use these proposed timing and support rules consistently.

      Before your class

      Sign in with your teacher access code. Choose your class, exam date, student, and time per part. The default is one minute per part. Allow about one extra minute for grading and changing students: about 12 students per hour before breaks. Adjust your timetable for your class.

      During each exam

      Check the student ID. Ask one main question and its follow-up in each of the four parts. Give the student a little time to think. You may repeat the question once using the same words. Avoid supplying answer words. If a topic does not fit the student's life, replace the question before the answer begins. Record extra help in the note if needed.

      Move on when the student finishes near the suggested time. The timer gives a cue, without cutting off speech. Pause it for an interruption. Use the same time and support rules throughout the class.

      Give the four marks

      Listen to the whole performance and choose the best matching description for each criterion. Do not count every error. Small mistakes and thinking pauses are allowed. Judge how easy the speech is to understand, without expecting a particular accent.

      Answer content is the original “Contribution” criterion. Grammar and words is the original “Accuracy” criterion. The weights remain 20%, 15%, 40%, and 25%. An absent student has no grade; a genuine zero is a recorded grade.

      Student instructions

      You will talk about yourself, your past, your life now, and your plans. You will answer a question and a short follow-up in each part. Try to give a reason or an example. You can ask, “Can you say that again, please?” If a question does not fit your life, tell your teacher.

      Example answers

      These are examples, not answers to memorize. Any relevant personal answer can be correct. Keep this guide in the teacher view during the exam.

      Save and reuse each year

      Your manager uploads the six-column roster and chooses a new exam session name each year. Teachers use their own access code and choose students from the list; names and IDs cannot be edited while starting an exam. Save each student's marks, then download a CSV and backup after each class. Browser storage belongs to this browser and this site address; it is not a cloud backup. Your backup includes all sessions. Restore merges records by record ID, keeping records already in this browser when IDs match.
